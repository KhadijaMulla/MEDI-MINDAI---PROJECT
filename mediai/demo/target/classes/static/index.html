The problem

Handwritten notes, Latin abbreviations and tiny print leave patients unsure what their prescription says. Missed or mixed-up doses follow.

The solution

MediMind AI rewrites prescription text in plain language, flags anything unclear for manual checking, and organizes medicines and reminders in one place.

In this demo, everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

MediMind AI does not diagnose, prescribe or suggest dose changes, and never fills in missing details. Always confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.
